4. Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are small data files that websites can store in your browser. Browser storage technologies, including localStorage and sessionStorage, can also save information on your device.

Depending on the features implemented, RexWeb Nexora may use browser storage to:

  • Remember your preferred light or dark theme.
  • Save game scores, settings, or progress on your device.
  • Remember certain interface preferences.
  • Support essential website functionality.

Data saved in local browser storage generally remains on your device unless it is transmitted elsewhere by a feature or script. Clearing your browser data may remove saved preferences and progress.

If analytics, advertising, or other third-party technologies are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies under their own settings and policies. Where required, consent will be requested before using non-essential technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain storage technologies may affect some website features.

5. Digital Tools, Converters, and Games

RexWeb Nexora may provide calculators, text utilities, converters, image tools, coding utilities, security tools, design tools, and browser-based games.

5.1 Browser-Based Processing

Some tools may process information directly in your browser. For example, a calculator can perform calculations locally, and a game can store a score in browser storage.

Where a tool operates entirely on your device, the data entered into that tool is not necessarily sent to our servers. However, this cannot be guaranteed for every tool without verifying its actual implementation and any connected third-party scripts.

5.2 File and Image Tools

If you use an image converter or another file-processing feature, the selected file may be processed in your browser or uploaded to a server, depending on how that feature has been implemented.

Before processing confidential or sensitive files, check the instructions shown by the specific tool. Do not assume that every file-processing feature works exclusively offline.

5.3 Coding and Text Tools

Text or code entered into a tool may remain in your browser when processing is local. If a feature connects to an external API, server, or third-party service, the submitted content may be transmitted to that service.

Avoid entering passwords, private keys, confidential source code, or sensitive personal information into tools unless the feature has been specifically designed and verified for that purpose.
