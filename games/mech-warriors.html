MECH WARRIORS

Fight through five dangerous sectors of the neon city. Destroy every regular enemy, defeat each sector boss, upgrade your mech, and claim the throne.

Campaign: 10, 20, 30, 40, then 50 regular enemies before each boss.

Difficulty: Normal · Pilot — balanced combat.

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the joystick. Aim with the mouse. Hold SPACE or FIRE to shoot. DASH gives a brief speed boost. EMP damages nearby enemies. Defeat the required regular enemies before the boss appears.
